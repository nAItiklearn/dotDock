dotDock 1.0.2 fixes what the first days of use turned up and starts you on a ready-made home screen. If you have
1.0.1, it installs from inside the app (Settings > About); otherwise download the installer or the portable exe below.

## What changed

- **Quick Settings closes again.** The panel the dock's status area opens now closes when you click elsewhere, press
  Esc or press the status area a second time.
- **A calmer dock.** Apps grow less under the cursor (at most 1.3×, 1.2× by default), about as much as category
  stacks do. A dock set higher before is brought down to the new limit.
- **A home screen from the first start**: clock, battery, music player, a photo frame for your own picture, timer,
  fun fact, memory and date, in a theme made from your wallpaper. The dock starts with the Obsidian bar, your
  recent apps and quick toggles.
- **The notch offers to connect Claude Code.** While nothing is running, the notch asks once whether to show Claude
  Code there; one click connects it, and you can disconnect in Settings > Notch. Codex tasks show with no setup.
- **Settings no longer freezes** when opened from the tray, the shortcut or a second launch.

The files are not signed with a code-signing certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time:
choose "More info", then "Run anyway". In-app updates are signed.
